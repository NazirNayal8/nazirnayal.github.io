# Layla fonts

LaylaThuluth.ttf and LaylaDiwani.ttf are unmodified copies of the Layla font collection, version 2.0,
by Mohammed Isam, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt, FONTLOG.txt and Authors).

Obtained from the ALT Linux package fonts-ttf-layla 2.0-alt1_2 (noarch RPMs fonts-ttf-layla-thuluth,
fonts-ttf-layla-diwani and fonts-ttf-layla-common), which lists the upstream home page as
http://sites.google.com/site/mohammedisam2000/home/projects.

They are used by assets/js/ambient.js for the calligraphy in the site background. The files must stay
unmodified to keep their Reserved Font Names (OFL FAQ 2.2 and 2.6).
