## Calligraphy in the background

The compositions that form in the side margins come from Wikimedia Commons. On this site they are recoloured and animated; the two under CC BY-SA are shared, as adapted here, under the same licences.

- Basmala, round composition, by Warraich Sahib. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bismillah_Calligraphy12.svg).
- Basmala, stacked composition, by Warraich Sahib. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bismillah_Calligraphy9.svg).
- Basmala in Thuluth, by Abdelghani Azzi. Public domain, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Basmalah-1wm.svg).
- <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">الْعُرْوَةُ الْوُثْقَى</span>, the masthead of the magazine Al-Urwah al-Wuthqa. Public domain, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A9_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AB%D9%82%D9%89_SVG_01.svg).
- Surah Al-Ikhlas, by the Wikimedia Commons user Economim. [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Al-ikhlas_calligraphy.svg).
- <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">الْقُرْآنُ الْكَرِيمُ</span>, by Habib M'henni. [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Quran_Kareem.svg).
